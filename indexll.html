About Me

I am a data and analytics professional with 8+ years of experience spanning data science, data engineering, customer analytics, AI/ML, and enterprise data architecture.

My career has progressively expanded from building analytical solutions to designing data platforms, shaping data products, and now working at the enterprise architecture level to connect business needs with data and technology strategy.

I am particularly interested in problems where the technical solution is only part of the challenge — understanding the business context, creating alignment across teams, establishing the right data model, and building an approach that can scale beyond a single use case.

In my current role, I work with business, product, and technical stakeholders to understand data landscapes, evaluate sources, develop conceptual and canonical models, and translate product needs into sustainable architecture.
